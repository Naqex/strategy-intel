How Does VSA Full Suite MT5 Work?

The software scans every single candle open-to-close in real-time, matching market mechanics into three variables:
💠 Volume (Effort): Categorized into 6 structural levels (Very Low to ULTRA) based on moving averages and percentiles.

💠 Spread (Result): Standardized via ATR volatility into Wide, Average, or Narrow profiles.

💠 Close (Position): Identifies intra-candle control via top (≥ 66%), middle, or bottom (≤ 33%) boundaries.

The algorithm instantly pairs these variables against the immediate structural backdrop to print 14 non-repaint VSA signals (ranging from Grade 1 exhaustions like No Supply/No Demand to Grade 3 major reversals like Selling Climax or Upthrust). Everything compiles into an automated VSA Score (0-100):

💠 Score ≥ 60: Strong Bullish Momentum ➡️ Long Setups Only.

💠 Score ≤ 40: Heavy Bearish Pressure ➡️ Short Setups Only.

💠 40 to 60 Range: Flat/Choppy Markets ➡️ Hands Off / Stand Aside.

Weaknesses and Risk Warnings

No trading engine is flawless, and the VSA Full Suite MT5 is not a holy grail. The ultimate limitation of any MT5 VSA tool on Forex pairs is the reliance on Tick Volume rather than centralized Real Volume. Consequently, low-liquidity environments can distort data, yielding false anomaly readings. Never open an order solely on an isolated Climax bar without a low-volume confirmation candle right behind it.

High-Impact Risk Warning: Disconnect all execution scripts 30 minutes before and after major macroeconomic releases (e.g., Non-Farm Payrolls - NFP, FOMC statements, CPI prints). During news spikes, institutions pull liquidity or sweep stops, temporarily breaking standard volume-spread relationships. This indicator is an institutional-tracking filter; it demands a solid understanding of Wyckoff principles and should never be used as a blind mechanical signal-box.
