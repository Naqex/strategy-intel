Overview

What Is Sunan Giri EA?

Sunan Giri EA is an automated trading system designed for the MetaTrader 4 (MT4) platform. The EA combines technical indicators with an adaptive grid recovery strategy to identify trading opportunities, manage positions, and respond to market fluctuations.

The system uses Bollinger Bands and Stochastic indicators to analyze volatility and momentum, while multi-timeframe confirmation is used to validate potential trading signals. Sunan Giri EA also incorporates adaptive compounding and configurable grid recovery, making it more suitable for experienced traders who understand the risks associated with grid-based trading.

Main Features

Sunan Giri EA combines Bollinger Bands and Stochastic indicators to identify volatility zones and momentum conditions for potential trade entries. The EA uses multi-timeframe confirmation to help filter trading signals and reduce random entries. Its adaptive compounding system can adjust position sizes as the account balance changes, while the configurable grid recovery mechanism allows the system to build layered positions during temporary drawdowns. The EA can automatically manage entries, averaging, and exits without manual intervention, providing a structured approach to automated trading.

Additional Features

Sunan Giri EA is designed to provide flexible automated trading through adjustable parameters for lot size, grid spacing, recovery depth, and risk management. This allows experienced users to configure the system according to their preferred trading conditions and account size.

The combination of technical indicators and grid recovery logic is intended to improve trade selection while providing a mechanism for managing temporary adverse market movements. However, the use of grid and averaging techniques means that exposure can increase when trades move against the initial position.

 

The test generated a reported $440 net profit from a $500 initial deposit, with a 65.01% win rate. However, the 48.86% maximum drawdown is very significant and indicates substantial historical exposure during unfavorable market conditions.

Therefore, the reported profitability should be evaluated together with the drawdown rather than considering the win rate or net profit alone.

Risk Management

Sunan Giri EA includes configurable risk controls that allow traders to adjust parameters such as lot size, grid spacing, and position depth. The adaptive compounding system can also modify position sizing as the account balance changes.

However, traders should pay particular attention to the risks associated with grid recovery and averaging. When the market continues moving against open positions, additional trades may increase total exposure and drawdown. The provided backtest recorded a maximum drawdown of 48.86%, which represents a very high level of historical risk.

For this reason, Sunan Giri EA should be used with conservative settings and sufficient account capital. Traders should never assume that the recovery mechanism can guarantee the recovery of losses.

How Sunan Giri EA Works

Sunan Giri EA begins by analyzing market conditions using Bollinger Bands and Stochastic indicators. These indicators help the system evaluate volatility, momentum, and potential entry conditions.

The EA then uses multi-timeframe confirmation to validate signals before entering trades. When a position moves against the expected direction, the adaptive grid recovery mechanism can add additional positions according to configured spacing and depth parameters.

The system automatically manages entries, averaging, recovery positions, and exits. Its adaptive compounding feature can also adjust position sizing according to changes in account balance.
