How Does STATARB FULL SUITE Work?

The core engine of STATARB FULL SUITE relies on the econometrics of cointegration rather than simple, unreliable price correlation.

The system executes a strict market neutral strategy. In macroeconomics, specific asset pairs maintain an inseparable economic bond, forcing them to move in parallel over long horizons (e.g., AUDUSD/NZDUSD or Gold/Silver). However, short-term shocks or localized order flow imbalances periodically force these assets into a temporary divergence. Guided by mathematical mean reversion, this price spread must eventually snap back to its historical equilibrium. The indicator exploits this structural rubber-band effect by building a two-legged trade: it simultaneously buys the relatively undervalued asset and shorts the overvalued asset. Profits are extracted solely from the convergence of the spread, completely shielding the portfolio from the broader market's directional trend.

To transform this economic logic into precise, non-discretionary execution, the underlying algorithm continuously tracks four core statistical metrics across a rolling 200-candle window:

  • Correlation Coefficient (ρ): Measures the linear strength between the log-prices of the two assets. The indicator filters out any pairs with a correlation below 0.70, prioritizing pairs with a ρ ≥ 0.85 for optimal structural alignment.
  • Beta Coefficient (β): Calculated via Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) regression. Beta dictates the exact hedge ratio required between the two legs. For instance, a Beta of 1.28 tells the system that a 1% move in asset B requires a 1.28% exposure shift in asset A to lock in true delta-neutrality.
  • Z-Score Spread: The raw price spread is modeled as Spread = log(A) - α - β ⋅ log(B). This value is continuously standardized into a Z-score. A z ≥ +2 indicates that asset A is unsustainably expensive relative to B, triggering a Short A / Long B plan. Conversely, a z ≤ -2 signals a Long A / Short B entry.
  • Half-Life (HL): Computed using an AR(1) autoregressive model on the spread. The Half-life defines the exact number of candles required for an extreme spread to decay halfway back to its mean. A half-life between 2 and 60 candles confirms reliable, tradeable mean reversion. A value of 999 acts as an immediate safety override, warning that the cointegration has completely broken down.

Key Features of STATARB FULL SUITE

All complex econometric computations are managed under the hood and fed into a rugged, high-information dashboard on your chart:

  • Multi-Pair Scanning Modes: Traders can toggle the ScanMode parameter between Chart vs List (matching the active chart symbol against a pre-defined array) or All Combinations (matrix scanning where an 8-symbol input automatically calculates and ranks all 28 synthetic pairs).
  • StatArb Score Engine: The system quantifies pair strength from 0 to 100. Points are mathematically weighted: up to 50 points for correlation strength, 25 points for valid Half-life bounds, 15 points for low Hurst exponent compression (Hurst < 0.45 indicates strong mean reversion), and 10 points for active Z-score extremity.
  • Dynamic Two-Legged Lot Sizing: The dashboard actively calculates the required volume for Leg B relative to Leg A's base lot (BaseLots). It integrates real-time tick values and asset specifications directly from your broker to ensure the hedge remains mathematically balanced.

Strict Threshold Parameters: The indicator features clear entry thresholds (𝑍𝑖𝑛=±2.0), partial or full take-profit targets when the spread compresses back to equilibrium (𝑍𝑜𝑢𝑡=±0.5), and an absolute statistical invalidation stop-loss (𝑍𝑠𝑡𝑜𝑝=±3.5).

  • Zero-Dependency Telegram Alerts: Utilizing the native Windows wininet.dll system library within MT5, the tool transmits real-time trade signals—including exact lot sizing, Z-scores, and correlation metrics—directly to your Telegram channel via custom TgToken and TgChatID inputs.

Step-by-Step Installation & Setup Guide

Follow this rigid 5-step technical blueprint to deploy and optimize the STATARB FULL SUITE on your MetaTrader 5 platform.

💻 Step 1: Deploy the Executable File

  1. Download the compiled file StatArbFullSuite_v1.0.ex5 from the secure area.
  2. Open your MT5 terminal, navigate to the top menu, and click File → Open Data Folder.
  3. Go into the following directory pathway: MQL5 → Indicators.
  4. Paste the StatArbFullSuite_v1.0.ex5 file directly into this folder.

🔄 Step 2: Refresh and Synchronize Terminal

  1. Return to the MT5 terminal interface.
  2. Locate the Navigator window (Press Ctrl + N if it is hidden).
  3. Right-click on the Indicators directory and select Refresh (or simply restart your MT5 terminal).

📊 Step 3: Populate Market Watch & Cache Historical Data

The underlying algorithm requires a minimum of 1,200 historical bars to construct valid OLS regression models and calculate the Hurst exponent.

  1. Go to the Market Watch window (Press Ctrl + M).
  2. Right-click inside the window and select Show All to ensure all target tickers specified in your PairList are visible.
  3. Crucial Action: Open the H1 chart for every single symbol in your target basket at least once. This forces the MT5 terminal to pull and cache the required historical data from the broker’s server.

🛠️ Step 4: Attach and Configure Parameters

  1. Drag and drop the StatArbFullSuite_v1.0 indicator from the Navigator window onto the H1 chart of your primary asset (Leg A).
  2. A properties window will pop up. Navigate to the Common tab and check the box for "Allow DLL imports". This is mandatory for the system to process advanced matrix telemetry and execute Telegram connection scripts.
  3. Switch to the Inputs tab and precisely define your parameters:
  • PairList: Type in your symbol names exactly as they appear in your broker’s Market Watch (pay close attention to suffixes, e.g., EURUSD.pro or XAUUSDm).
  • ScanMode: Set to Chart vs List or All Combinations depending on your exposure strategy.
  • Zin / Zout / Zstop: Keep at the optimized defaults (2.0 / 0.5 / 3.5) or tighten to 2.5 for high-volatile sessions.

🤖 Step 5: Automate Live Telegram Telemetry

To stream real-time correlation break alerts and Z-score extremes directly to your server or phone, execute the API handshake:

  1. Connect with @BotFather on Telegram, send the /newbot command, and copy the generated API Token.
  2. Start the bot by clicking Start, then use @userinfobot to retrieve your personal Chat ID.
  3. In the indicator’s Inputs interface, scroll down to Group 7 (Telegram):
  • Toggle TgEnable to true.
  • Paste your unique credentials into TgToken and TgChatID.
  1. Click OK to close the properties window. Locate the "TEST TG" button at the footer of the active chart dashboard and click it to confirm a successful connection handshake.

Weaknesses and Risk Warnings

In quantitative finance, there is no such thing as a risk-free edge. Traders must explicitly account for the following structural vulnerabilities:

The apex threat to this strategy is structural regime breakdown (Co-integration Failure). While two assets may maintain an identical economic trajectory for decades, a sudden macroeconomic divergence—such as an unannounced, aggressive interest rate hike by one central bank while the other remains dovish—can snap the historical bond permanently. If a trader ignores the statistical boundaries and fails to liquidate positions when the Z-score breaches the absolute invalidation threshold (

𝑍𝑠𝑡𝑜𝑝=±3.5), the spread can widen exponentially, resulting in severe capital drawdowns.

Secondly, negative swap accumulation poses a silent threat to profitability. Because mean reversion requires time to materialize (often spanning multiple days depending on the Half-life), carrying two opposing high-volume legs across daily rolls can accumulate significant swap fees. If the broker's swap rates are heavily punitive, these fees can completely erode the positive mathematical expectancy (+0.31% average gain) of the trade.

Finally, execution slippage introduces severe math distortion if trades are entered manually. The foundation of a market-neutral pair trade requires both legs to be filled within split-seconds of each other. If extreme market volatility causes an execution delay or slippage on one leg, the precise beta-hedged ratio is compromised. This transforms a protected, market-blind spread trade into a highly exposed, directional gamble. Past in-sample backtest simulations serve as mathematical benchmarks but never guarantee future performance under live, dynamic market conditions.
