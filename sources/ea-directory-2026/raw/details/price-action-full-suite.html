How Does Price Action Full Suite Work?

Price Action Full Suite operates strictly as a semi-automated data visualization indicator suite, NOT a 100% automated trading bot (Auto-bot). Its primary algorithmic engine strips away psychological bias by converting price mechanics into actionable on-chart structural data.

[Raw Price Data] ➔ [ATR Volatility Filter] ➔ [EMA 20/25 Trend Core] ➔ [On-Chart Swing Mapping]
  • Algorithmic Market Mapping: The software runs a continuous multi-bar scan based on custom lookback parameters (PivLen) to instantly lock and label critical market swing points: HH (Higher High), HL (Higher Low), LH (Lower High), and LL (Lower Low).
  • Dynamic Trend Filtering: It utilizes a calibrated EMA 20 (or EMA 25) as a fluid benchmark for trend direction and momentum. When price gaps away from the EMA by a statistically significant margin, the trend state updates instantly.
  • Automatic Range Detection: When price actions stall and trap between matching horizontal extremes, the algorithm draws an automated Trading Range Box on your chart. This acts as a visual trigger for mean-reversion setups.
  • Real-Time Candlestick Translation: The core math engine calculates individual bar metrics (body-to-wick ratios mapped against current ATR volatility) to display live textual market state readings on a clean heads-up dashboard:
    • Trend Bars: Body represents ≥ 60% of total range → Displays: "Strong Buying/Selling Pressure".
    • Reversal Bars: Tail/Wick represents ≥ 60% of total range → Displays: "Price Rejection Verified".
    • Doji Bars: Tight body ≤ 20% of total range → Displays: "Market Indecision - Stand Aside".

Weaknesses and Risk Warnings

High-Risk Financial Disclaimer: Financial derivative and margin trading carry substantial risk of total capital loss. Price Action Full Suite does not guarantee future profitability or immunity from market losses.

  • Consolidation Failure Points: During highly compressed, low-volume consolidation squeeze phases (Transition States), the algorithm will occasionally print rapid alternating swing structures. This increases the risk of being whipped out if trading blindly without macro volume filters.
  • High-Impact News Breaches: Extreme macroeconomic event releases (e.g., NFP, CPI, FOMC decisions) break technical structures due to institutional order book vacuuming, slippage, and spread widening. Do not execute new setups 10 minutes before and after major news items.
  • Strict Order Execution Mandate: To neutralize false breakouts, operators must completely abandon standard Market Orders. Trades should strictly be deployed using Brooks-style Stop Orders placed 1 tick beyond the signal bar extreme (Buy Stop above the high; Sell Stop below the low). If the next bar fails to trigger the entry and breaks backwards, the setup is instantly voided, saving your account capital.
