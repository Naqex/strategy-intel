How Does EAFree TradePad v2.16 Work?

Unlike automated robots that scan for trading signals, EAFree TradePad v2.16 is an MT5 scalping tool that functions as a professional on-chart control panel, focusing entirely on facilitating manual trade entry and automating risk management. The core operating mechanism of this free money management EA lies in its highly optimized lot size calculation system. Specifically, the system automatically determines the trade volume based on the user-defined risk percentage (Risk %) relative to the account balance and the stop-loss (SL) distance.

A breakthrough feature that makes this panel a powerful XAUUSDm Gold trading tool is its automatic pip unit detection mechanism (InpPipMode Auto). This feature dynamically analyzes and standardizes pip values ​​based on the asset's actual price volatility, remaining completely independent of the broker's decimal digit settings. Thanks to this intelligent algorithm, the software effectively handles digit discrepancies across different platforms, ensuring that calculations for spreads and stop-loss levels remain absolutely accurate on specific account configurations—such as Exness's XAUUSDm—regardless of whether the broker uses a 2-digit or 3-digit format.

How to Install EAFree TradePad v2.16

Follow these precise steps to install and configure the EA on your MetaTrader 5 platform:

💠Step 1: Open your MT5 terminal, click on the File menu, select Open Data Folder, and navigate to the MQL5\Experts\ directory.

💠Step 2: Copy the entire EAFree_TradePad_v2.16 folder into this directory. Ensure the exact folder path is MQL5\Experts\EAFree_TradePad_v2.16\EAFree TradePad v2.16.mq5 and that all accompanying .mqh files are included.

💠Step 3: Press F4 to open the MetaEditor, open the EAFree TradePad_v2.16.mq5 file, and press F7 to Compile. The expected compilation result should display "0 errors".

💠Step 4: Return to your MT5 terminal, locate EAFree_TradePad v2.16 in the Navigator panel, and drag it onto your trading chart. Ensure that you turn on Algo Trading (the button on the top toolbar must be green).

💠Step 5 (Optional Settings):

➡️Telegram integration: Go to Tools > Options > Expert Advisors, check the box for Allow WebRequest for listed URL, and add [https://api.telegram.org](https://api.telegram.org).

➡️Push notifications: Go to Tools > Options > Notifications and input your MetaQuotes ID from the MT5 mobile app.

⚠️*Important Note: The panel operates strictly on the chart it is attached to, meaning you need one panel per chart. By default, the panel is designed to manage only the active trades of that specific chart's symbol.*

Weaknesses and Risk Warnings

Users must be particularly aware of critical technical risks when operating this control panel. Features such as hidden SL/TP and automated functions (e.g., trailing stops, partial profit-taking) only remain active while the MT5 platform and the EA are running. If the computer loses its internet connection or the software shuts down, all open positions will be left without any protective safeguards on the broker's server. Furthermore, if the system-calculated trade volume (based on risk percentage) falls below the broker's minimum lot requirement, the order size will be automatically adjusted up to that minimum. This discrepancy results in an actual risk level that is significantly higher than the risk originally intended by the user.
