Overview

FRACTAL / IPDA SUITE is a premium technical analysis system built for MetaTrader 5 (MT5), designed specifically for traders executing institutional Smart Money Concepts (SMC) and Inner Circle Trader (ICT) methodologies. Far from a basic, generic script, this advanced indicator suite completely mechanizes market structure. It integrates price fractal geometry with the Interbank Price Delivery Algorithm (IPDA) to track where real bank liquidity is moving. Engineered for maximum efficiency on high-liquidity assets like Gold (XAUUSD), major Forex pairs, and US Indices, it forces a strict multi-timeframe workflow. Traders use the daily chart (D1) to lock in the directional Daily Bias, then execute sniper entries with ultra-tight stop losses on M3 or M5 charts.

How Does FRACTAL / IPDA SUITE Work?

The core engine operates on the mathematical law of market fractals: price action structures on the micro-charts mirror the exact algorithmic distribution models found on the macro-charts. The suite automates this entire analytical pipeline through a strict, 4-step state machine:

  • Step 1 - Algorithmic Daily Bias: At exactly 00:00 server time, the system samples the newly closed D1 candle to filter the institutional direction for the upcoming sessions. It classifies market states into three clear categories: Liquidity Sweep-and-Reverse (highest probability), Trend Expansion, or an Inside Day consolidation.
  • Step 2 - IPDA Coordinates & D1 FVG Mapping: The algorithm auto-calculates current Dealing Ranges using 20, 40, and 60-day lookback data blocks. It partitions your chart into definitive Premium (sell only) and Discount (buy only) zones, while overlaying unmitigated Daily Fair Value Gaps (FVGs) directly onto your execution screen.
  • Step 3 - Draw on Liquidity (DOL) Targeting: The suite flags the specific structural targets that algorithms are highly likely to hunt next—such as Previous Daily Highs/Lows (PDH/PDL) or Previous Weekly Highs/Lows (PWH/PWL). This DOL serves as a price magnet and defines your ultimate Take Profit barrier.
  • Step 4 - Lower Timeframe (M5) Confirmation Trigger: Once macro conditions align, the lower timeframe state machine activates, scanning for a precise three-tier reversal sequence:
    1. Liquidity Sweep: Price raids a key swing point, flagged instantly by a yellow diamond icon.
    2. Displacement: An aggressive, high-momentum candle (minimum 1.2 ATR) blasts through, creating a clean execution FVG.
    3. Market Structure Shift (MSS): Price closes past the recent swing point, confirming an official institutional shift.

How to Install FRACTAL / IPDA SUITE on MT5?

Deploying the FRACTAL / IPDA SUITE onto your MetaTrader 5 terminal is a straightforward physical setup process. Follow these 5 deployment steps:

  • Step 1 - Copy the Execution File (.ex5): Go to your MT5 top navigation menu, click File -> Open Data Folder. Navigate to the MQL5 folder, then open the Indicators directory. Copy the FractalFullSuite_v1.0.ex5 file directly into this folder.
  • Step 2 - Refresh the System Navigator: Return to your MT5 workspace. Open the Navigator window (Ctrl + N), right-click on the Indicators tree, and click Refresh (or simply restart your MT5 terminal).
  • Step 3 - Grant System DLL Permissions: Drag and drop the indicator from the Navigator window onto your active lower timeframe execution chart (M3, M5, or M15). In the configuration pop-up window, switch to the Common tab and check the box for Allow DLL imports. Click OK to initialize.
  • Step 4 - Force Historical Data Sync: Once attached, flip your chart quickly to the D1 and W1 timeframes for a few seconds. This forces MT5 to fetch all historical data blocks, which allows the IPDA engine to accurately calculate its 20/40/60-day lookback metrics.
  • Step 5 - Set Language Preference: Check the dashboard interface overlay on your chart. Click directly on the EN/VI button located at the bottom corner (Footer) to switch the complete user interface to your preferred language.

Weaknesses and Risk Warnings

High-performance trading demands absolute transparency regarding risk. Users must master the specific technical limitations of this strategy:

  • Aggressive Short-Term Stop Outs: Because execution stops (SL) are pinned tightly to M5 structures, you will experience consecutive losses during highly volatile, noisy market conditions. Success requires an unshakeable mindset, strict acceptance of probability, and zero premature trade closures before targets hit.
  • Hard Broker Server Múi Giờ Requirements: The Daily Bias engine relies heavily on accurate daily candle closes. You must run this suite on a broker using standard New York close charts (typically GMT+3 in summer, GMT+2 in winter). Incorrect server times will corrupt the data and generate false bias signals.
  • Zero-Tolerance Leverage Limits: Do not abuse tight stops to oversize your lot allocations. Best practices require a fixed risk ceiling of 0.5% to 1% of your total equity per trade. Furthermore, caps must be set at a maximum of 2 failed execution attempts per day on any single bias direction to eliminate overtrading and emotional revenge trading.
