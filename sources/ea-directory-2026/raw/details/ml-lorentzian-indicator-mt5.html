How Does ML Lorentzian Indicator MT5 Work?

The ML Lorentzian Indicator MT5 completely abandons rigid, fixed-formula calculations like traditional EMAs or RSIs. Instead, it utilizes a live KNN (K-Nearest Neighbors) classification algorithm that learns dynamically from the chart's historical data.

[Current Candle Features] 
       │
       ▼ (Scans past 2,000 bars via Lorentzian Distance)
[Finds 8 Closest Historical Neighbors]
       │
       ▼ (Analyzes Win/Loss outcome of those neighbors)
[Generates Real-Time Directional Probability]
  • Lorentzian Distance Metric: Standard models use Euclidean distance (straight-line), which gets heavily distorted by market spikes. This indicator uses the Lorentzian function: $\ln(1 + \vert{}\Delta\vert{})$. By flattening extreme price anomalies caused by high-impact news, the model successfully identifies accurate historical "neighbors" even during intense market volatility.
  • Walk-Forward Self-Tuning: The market constantly shifts regimes. Every 500 candles (TuneEvery), the system automatically backtests 12 distinct configurations across a 600-candle window (TuneWindow). It instantly locks in the highest-performing K-neighbors and feature weights for the upcoming price action.
  • Nadaraya-Watson Kernel Regression: Operating as a dynamic trend filter, this rational quadratic kernel functions as a smooth, ultra-responsive baseline. It prints green during structural uptrends and red during downtrends, serving as the ultimate trend-safeguard.

Key Features of ML Lorentzian Indicator MT5

The AI digitizes the chart by evaluating 5 standardized inputs normalized between 0% and 100%:

⚠️ The 4-Layer Execution Filters

An arrow signal is printed only when all active filters grant permission:

  • Regime Filter: Kernel slope must be > -0.1 to strictly block buying into vertical crashes.
  • ADX Filter: Optional trend filter ensuring ADX > 20 before execution.
  • Kernel Filter: AI prediction direction must match the current Kernel line color.

How to Install and Set Up ML Lorentzian Full Suite MT5

Follow this straightforward, step-by-step guide to get the AI indicator running on your MetaTrader 5 platform within 3 minutes.

📌 Step 1: Copy the File to MT5 Directory

  1. Download the file MLLorentzianFullSuite_v1.0.ex5.
  2. Open your MetaTrader 5 platform.
  3. Click on File in the top menu → Select Open Data Folder.
  4. Navigate through the folders: MQL5 → Indicators.
  5. Paste the downloaded .ex5 file into this folder.

🔄 Step 2: Refresh the Navigator

  1. Go back to MT5 and look at the Navigator window (Press Ctrl + N if it is hidden).
  2. Right-click on the Indicators folder → Click Refresh (or simply restart your MT5).
  3. You will now see MLLorentzianFullSuite_v1.0 in the list.

📊 Step 3: Apply to Your Chart

  1. Open a chart of your choice (Recommended: XAUUSD, Major Forex, or Crypto).
  2. Set the timeframe to M15, M30, or H1. (Ensure your chart has at least 6,000 bars of historical data).
  3. Drag and drop the indicator from the Navigator onto the chart.

⚠️ Step 4: Enable DLL Imports (Critical)

  1. When the settings window pops up, click on the Common tab.
  2. Check the box "Allow DLL imports".
  • Note: This is mandatory because the system utilizes the Windows wininet.dll library to push real-time alerts straight to your Telegram bot.
  1. Click OK. The AI model will take a few seconds to calculate the first 3,000 bars and optimize itself.

🛠️ Step 5: Verify the Model Health

  1. Look at the Experts tab at the bottom of your MT5 terminal.
  2. You should see a success log printed: "ML Lorentzian: bars=... K=8 preset=... samples=... acc=...% trades=...".
  3. If the Rolling Accuracy on the dashboard shows < 50%, switch to a higher timeframe or turn off weak features to let the model re-sync.

Weaknesses and Risk Warnings

We must dissect the architectural limits of this machine learning system:

  1. Macroeconomic Blindspot: The AI reads pure price data. It cannot comprehend the economic calendar. Trading blindly into high-impact news releases (Non-Farm Payrolls, CPI, FOMC decisions) will result in stopped-out trades due to severe slippage and spread widening.
  2. Daily Chart Decay (D1): Running this model on a D1 timeframe requires a 2,000-candle lookback, which spans roughly 8 years. Market regimes change drastically over decades, making long-term past data completely irrelevant to current macro cycles.
  3. The Probability Trap: A 75% neighbor agreement score printed on the chart does not equal a 75% future win rate. It simply means 6 out of 8 past instances moved in that direction. If the rolling accuracy metric drops below 50%, the model is out of sync and must be paused immediately.
