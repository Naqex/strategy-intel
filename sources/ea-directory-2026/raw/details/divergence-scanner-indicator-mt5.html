How Does Divergence Scanner Indicator MT5 Work?

The underlying engine relies on a strict, mathematical asset tracking logic that eliminates regular human analytical bias. Instead of chasing lagging indicators, the system maps market structure using a fixed 5-bar Swing Pivot rule (PivLen = 5). By locking lines on the chart only after the 5th confirming candle completes, the system provides 100% No-Repaint signals that remain permanently fixed on your chart.

The core algorithm measures three distinct market forces simultaneously by processing 5 advanced oscillators every single candle:

  • Price Momentum: Calculated via a classic RSI 14 (Wilder method).
  • Trend Momentum: Tracked via the core MACD line to capture sustained swing moves.
  • Volume & Active Capital Flow: Deciphered via OBV, MFI 14, and CVD (Cumulative Volume Delta). The CVD engine processes raw tick data within a rolling 24-hour cache to expose aggressive, real-time institutional buying and selling pressure.

To provide immediate actionable insight, the indicator aggregates these data points into a single Divergence Score (0–100). This score automatically weights the freshness, class tier, and indicator confluence of active setups within the past 60 bars. When the system detects an M15 setup aligning with a higher-timeframe H1 divergence, it flags a (Synchronized MTF) state—triggering a high-conviction momentum exhaustion alert.

Key Features of Divergence Scanner Indicator MT5

  • Structural Class Tiering: Categorizes signals into Class A (Strongest: new price extreme vs. higher indicator extreme), Class B (Double top/bottom price vs. higher indicator extreme), Class C (New price extreme vs. flat indicator extreme), and Hidden (H) continuation setups.
