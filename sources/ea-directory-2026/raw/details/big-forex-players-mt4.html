Overview

What Is Big Forex Players MT4?

Big Forex Players EA is an automated trading system developed for MetaTrader 4 (MT4), designed to combine multiple trading strategies within a single Expert Advisor. The system provides three primary approaches that can be used independently or simultaneously: Trading with Banks, Trading with Indicators, and Trading with Robots.

The Bank strategy uses positions based on research and analysis of major banks and investment funds, with signals delivered from an external database to the EA. The Indicator strategy generates positions using popular technical indicators such as RSI, MACD, and Stochastic, while the Robot strategy combines Price Action, Cycle Strength, SMA Strength, RSI, and additional built-in indicators.

Big Forex Players EA is designed to support both individual Forex traders and Prop Trading Firm users, with extensive customization options for risk management, news filtering, trading hours, position sizing, and trade management.

Main Features

Big Forex Players EA combines three distinct trading approaches that traders can enable separately or use simultaneously. The Trading with Banks strategy uses positions based on research and analysis of major banks and investment funds, while the Trading with Indicators strategy generates signals from RSI, MACD, and Stochastic indicators on higher timeframes such as H4 and D1. The Trading with Robots strategy uses Price Action, Cycle Strength, SMA Strength, RSI, and additional built-in indicators, with Low, Medium, and High risk modes. The EA also includes automated money management, configurable lot sizing, trailing stops, Take Profit and Stop Loss controls, spread filtering, trading-hour settings, and a comprehensive news filter.

Additional Features

Big Forex Players EA provides extensive customization options for traders who want greater control over automated trading. Each major strategy can be enabled or disabled with a simple true/false parameter, allowing users to select the approach that best fits their trading objectives.

The EA also includes a detailed trading panel displaying information such as daily profit, daily pips, current trading session, Forex calendar, and spread. Traders can manually close positions directly from the panel when necessary.

The system is available for MetaTrader 4 and MetaTrader 5 and is designed to work with various account types, brokers, and Prop Trading Firms. A detailed manual is provided in multiple languages, and the developer offers dedicated customer support and ongoing updates.

Risk Management

Big Forex Players EA includes multiple risk-management features designed to help traders control exposure. Users can define a Risk Percent, choose between Low, Medium, and High risk modes, configure lot sizes, adjust order spacing, and use trailing stops.

The EA also provides Maximum Spread filtering, Stop Loss, Take Profit, account-balance-based loss controls, and an option to disable new trades after a predefined maximum drawdown loss. These features are designed to reduce exposure during unfavorable market conditions.

An integrated news filter can block new trades around high-impact economic events. According to the provided settings, the EA can prevent new positions for 60 minutes before and 60 minutes after important news. Traders can also select specific currencies and keywords for the news filter.

Despite these controls, automated trading remains risky. The provided performance data shows a low historical drawdown, but the extremely high reported return should be treated cautiously and independently verified.

How Big Forex Players EA Works

Big Forex Players EA operates using three core trading strategies. The first strategy, Trading with Banks, receives trading positions based on research and analysis of major banks and investment funds. These signals are delivered from the developer's database to the EA for automated execution.

The second strategy, Trading with Indicators, generates trading signals using RSI, MACD, and Stochastic indicators, primarily on H4 and D1 charts. The third strategy, Trading with Robots, combines Price Action, Cycle Strength, SMA Strength, RSI, and additional custom indicators to identify trading opportunities.

Once a trading signal is generated, the EA can automatically execute and manage positions using predefined Stop Loss and Take Profit levels. Traders can select different risk modes and customize lot sizing, trading hours, order spacing, trailing stops, spread limits, and news filters according to their preferences.
