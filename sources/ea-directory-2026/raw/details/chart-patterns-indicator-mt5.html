Overview

Maximize your trading efficiency and eliminate emotional bias. CHART PATTERNS Indicator MT5 is a non-repaint automated chart pattern MT5 indicator engineered for high-performance traders. Optimized for Gold (XAUUSD), major Forex pairs, Crypto, and Indices, this tool scans multiple timeframes—detecting major macro shifts on H1–H4 and explosive continuation setups on M15.

How Does CHART PATTERNS Indicator MT5 Work?

The indicator processes market structure through a mathematical, non-repaint framework.

  • Objective Pivot Detection: Pinpoints swing highs/lows using a strict PivLen (6-bar) configuration to eliminate human error.
  • Volatility ATR Filters: Filters market noise by requiring a strict peak/valley alignment (EqualTolATR = 0.6) and a minimum pattern height (MinHeightATR ≥ 2.0).
  • 4-Stage State Machine:
    1. Forming: Tracks structural building. Automatically expires after 120 bars (ExpireBars) if no breakout occurs.
    2. Breakout: Triggered the exact moment a candle closes outside the trendline or neckline.
    3. Measured Move: Automatically projects Take Profit (TP) at 1.0H (pattern height) and sets Stop Loss (SL) at a strict 0.5H invalidation level.

Result: Logs hard data points—Target Hit (✓✓✓) or Failed (×). Expired at 150 bars (TargetBars) if flatlined.

Key Features of CHART PATTERNS Indicator MT5

  • 12-Pattern Auto-Scanning: Instantly maps out 7 core pattern families: Head & Shoulders, Double Top/Bottom, Triangles (Ascending/Descending/Symmetrical), Wedges, Channels, Flags, and Cup & Handle.
  • Live Performance Dashboard: Displays historical sample sizes, target hit win-rates, and average bars to TP directly on your active chart.
  • Hard Backtest Engine: Instantly evaluates up to 3,000 historical bars (MaxHistory) upon loading to calculate true asset edge.
  • Instant Alerts: Fires on-screen visual arrows, native MT5 audio alerts, and direct Telegram notifications via Windows wininet.dll without any external expert advisors.

How to Install and Setup CHART PATTERNS Indicator MT5

Follow this fast 5-step deployment guide to get the automated scanner running on your MetaTrader 5 platform:

  • Step 1: Move the File: Open your MT5 terminal. Go to File → Open Data Folder. Navigate to MQL5 → Indicators. Paste the ChartPatternsFullSuite_v1.0.ex5 file into this folder.
  • Step 2: Refresh MT5: Go to the Navigator window (or press Ctrl + N). Right-click on Indicators and click Refresh to load the tool.
  • Step 3: Enable DLL Imports: Drag and drop the indicator onto your preferred chart (e.g., XAUUSD H1). In the Common tab, you must check the box for "Allow DLL imports". Note: This is mandatory for the Telegram alert system to function.
  • Step 4: Verify Activation: Check the Experts log tab at the bottom of your terminal. It should print: "Chart Patterns: pivots=… patterns=… targets hit=…".
  • Step 5: Select Language: The dashboard defaults to English. If needed, simply click the EN/VI button located at the footer of the live dashboard to toggle your language preference.

Weaknesses and Risk Warnings

  • Inherent Execution Lag: Zero-repaint guarantees require a trade-off. The indicator must wait for the PivLen (6 bars) to close to confirm a valid swing point, meaning patterns print a few candles after the absolute structural peak/valley.
  • Lower Timeframe Noise: Scalping timeframes (M1–M5) generate heavy micro-noise and fractured structures. Users must tighten settings by increasing PivLen to 8–10.
  • Risk Disclaimer: Simulated performance results have certain inherent limitations. Historical win-rates do not guarantee future profit margins. Trading Forex, CFDs, and Crypto involves substantial risk of capital loss.
