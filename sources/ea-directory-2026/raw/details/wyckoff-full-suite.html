Overview

The Wyckoff Full Suite EA MT5 from eafree.net is a high-caliber technical analysis system engineered to expose institutional order flow across major financial arenas, including XAUUSD (Gold), Forex, and Crypto. Far from being a lagging tool, this automated Wyckoff structure suite decodes pure price action and volume characteristics to target high-probability accumulation and distribution zones in real time. How Does Wyckoff Full Suite Work?

The algorithmic engine maps the market using a mathematical Pivot Length (defaulted to 8 candles) fused with Average True Range (ATR) normalization. This structural synergy defines the exact boundaries of the Trading Range—specifically the Creek (resistance) and the Ice (support)—guaranteeing strict non-repaint execution on MetaTrader 5.

The system tracks the 4 key phases of the Composite Man:

  • Accumulation: Quiet institutional buying at major discount zones.

  • Markup: Bullish expansion and public trend continuation.
  • Distribution: Institutional liquidations and profit-taking at premium prices.

  • Markdown: Bearish capitulation following structural failure.

To streamline complex market calculations, the suite introduces the Wyckoff Score (0–100). This data matrix synthesizes the 50 EMA trend slope, localized Buying/Selling Volume Pressure, and current structural phases over a custom candle lookback (StatLook). A Wyckoff Score \ge 60 signals a Bullish regime (buy filters only), while a score \le 40 confirms a Bearish market (sell filters only), keeping your bias perfectly aligned with smart money momentum.

Key Features of Wyckoff Full Suite

  • Automated Structure Labeling: Automatically targets and anchors key milestones like Selling Climax (SC), Buying Climax (BC), Automatic Rally (AR), Secondary Test (ST), Spring, UTAD, SOS, and SOW immediately upon candle close.
  • Advanced VSA Engine: Maps raw transactional effort directly against price results to reveal immediate candlestick setups, including No Supply (NS), No Demand (ND), Test Low, Stopping Volume (SV), and Effort vs Result (E>R) traps.
  • Dynamic Range Analytics: Tracks the background accumulation timeline (Cause) and projects institutional price targets (Target) using time-tested mathematical measured move formulas.
  • Risk Management Integration: Integrates an automated "Range FAIL" mechanism that instantly triggers if a trade location breaks in the opposing direction on heavy volume, forcing an objective structural reset.
  • Multi-Channel Alerts: Delivers critical data streams directly via desktop Popups, mobile Push notifications, or instant real-time Telegram alerts using native Windows wininet.dll library connections.

Weaknesses and Risk Warnings

No system can eliminate market volatility entirely. Traders must recognize the core structural risks to protect active capital:

  • The Phase B Pitfall: Phase B accounts for up to 80% of a range’s lifespan. Trading inside this zone exposes accounts to erratic price swings and false upthrusts engineered to trigger early retail stop-losses.
  • The Pivot Lag Factor: To eliminate repaint anomalies, secondary markers (AR, ST, LPS) require confirmation and will post with an intentional lag exactly equal to your configured Pivot Length.
  • Tick Volume Distortions: Spot Forex and Gold lack a centralized exchange; therefore, the system utilizes broker-specific Tick Volume. Low-liquidity environments can skew VSA accuracy. Do not execute new trades 30 minutes before or after major high-impact news (NFP, FOMC, CPI). Leverage amplified trading carries substantial financial risk.

How to Install and Setup Wyckoff Full Suite on MT5

Setting up the Wyckoff Full Suite on MetaTrader 5 is a straightforward process. Because this system relies on secure external connections to feed your live dashboard and send instant trade alerts, following the exact installation steps is critical to unlocking its full mathematical power.

Follow this definitive step-by-step blueprint to configure the eafree.net Wyckoff indicator on your MT5 platform:

Step 1: Deploy the Indicator Files

  1. Download the core file WyckoffFullSuite.ex5 from your eafree.net dashboard.
  2. Open your MetaTrader 5 terminal. In the top menu, navigate to File ➡️ Open Data Folder.
  3. A Windows directory will pop up. Open the MQL5 folder, then double-click into the Indicators subfolder.
  4. Copy and paste the WyckoffFullSuite.ex5 file directly into this location.

Step 2: Refresh and Mount to the Chart

  1. Return to your MT5 terminal and look at the Navigator window (Press Ctrl + N if it is hidden).
  2. Right-click on the Indicators heading and select Refresh. The WyckoffFullSuite will now appear in your list.
  3. Open your target chart—such as XAUUSD on the M15 or H1 timeframe.
  4. Drag and drop the indicator from the Navigator window straight onto your active chart.

Step 3: Grant Essential System Permissions

  • CRITICAL FOR TELEGRAM ALERTS: Before clicking OK, switch over to the Common tab in the configuration window.
  • Locate the Allow DLL imports checkbox and ensure it is checked ✅.
  • Why this matters: The suite utilizes secure, native Windows DLL libraries (wininet.dll and shell32.dll) to seamlessly bypass complex third-party software and broadcast real-time data streams straight to your devices.

Step 4: Configure Input Parameters & Notifications

  1. Navigate to the Inputs tab to refine your operational settings:
  • Language Tuning: The default language is English, but you can immediately toggle between English and Vietnamese by clicking the EN/VI button located right on the live chart's footer dashboard.
  • Alert Channels: Locate Group 5 (Alerts) to switch on AlertPopup for desktop sound queues and AlertPush to feed data directly to your mobile MT5 app via your MetaQuotes ID.
  1. Click OK to render the automated Wyckoff structure and live dashboard on your screen.

Step 5: Establish the Live Telegram Stream (Optional)

To instantly receive critical Phase C/D structural triggers (Spring, UTAD, SOS, SOW) directly inside your phone's Telegram app:

  1. Open Telegram, locate @BotFather, type /newbot, and copy your unique Bot Token. Press Start on your newly created bot.
  2. Search for @userinfobot to grab your personal numerical Chat ID.
  3. Go back to your MT5 chart, hit Inputs, scroll down to Group 6 (Telegram), set TgEnable to true, and paste both your Token and Chat ID.
  4. To test the connection, click the gold TEST TG button on your chart's footer panel. A verified confirmation text will hit your phone immediately.
