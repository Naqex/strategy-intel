Overview

Scalping Robot Pro MT5 is a fully automated trading system developed for MetaTrader 5. It trades the XAUUSD pair exclusively on the M1 timeframe.

The gold market presents massive volatility daily. Sudden price spikes easily trigger emotional errors for human traders. This software monitors the market to execute fast trades without human panic.

How Does Scalping Robot Pro MT5 Work?

The software scans candle momentum on the one-minute chart. It utilizes the Parabolic SAR indicator to identify immediate trend directions. It couples this with an Average True Range filter using a 14 period setting. This combination helps find fast market entries during stable volatility levels.

However, the core logic relies on an underlying grid layout. The system permits a maximum of five open positions at once. It maintains a tight distance of 40 pips between these orders. If the gold price moves against the initial trade, it activates a 1.89 martingale multiplier. The software drops fixed target levels to handle these basket orders active on the chart.

For event security, a remote server tracks specific social feeds. It monitors prominent accounts like Donald Trump and the White House. When geopolitical text triggers warnings, the system suspends new trades for 60 to 120 minutes. A similar news filter halts activity around high-impact USD economic events.

Key Features of Scalping Robot Pro MT5

  • Asset Focus: The system operates exclusively on the XAUUSD gold spot contract. Traders cannot apply this software to foreign exchange pairs or indices.
  • Timeframe: The coding works solely on the M1 chart for rapid trade execution. Running it on higher timeframes will disrupt the internal trigger settings.
  • Min Deposit: The developer recommends a minimum starting balance of $1,000 for safety. Lower balances face immediate margin pressure during regular grid scaling events.
  • Account Type: Users must utilize ECN, RAW, or zero spread account setups. High retail spreads will degrade the performance of this automated system.
  • Leverage: The operational settings support account leverage from 1:20 up to 1:1000. Higher leverage remains necessary to sustain multiple martingale positions.
  • File Format: The delivery package contains compiled .ex5 files and optimized .set configuration files. Users can load these files directly into the MT5 platform.

Performance & Live Signal Results

The vendor provides brief statistics from backtest logs and live accounts. The three-month backtest report shows an unverified net profit of $27,797.27 from a $1,000 deposit. The report claims an equity drawdown of 3.65% with a 5.78 profit factor. Meanwhile, the five-week live signal shows 115.23% growth on a $500 account with a 20.5% drawdown.

Pros and Cons of Scalping Robot Pro MT5

✅Pros

  • The integrated news protection successfully pauses trading before major economic releases.
  • Geopolitical tracking helps avoid unexpected market gaps during major political announcements.
  • The system enforces strict time controls to avoid low liquidity sessions.
  • Traders can configure individual parameters to restrict daily account exposure levels.

❌Cons

  • The 1.89 martingale multiplier creates an immense threat to the trading capital.
  • Total reliance on dynamic exits means bad execution can cause massive losses.
  • High reliance on broker conditions makes it vulnerable to sudden spread widening.
  • Traders cannot easily diversify risks since it only operates on gold charts.

Weaknesses and Risk Warnings

The primary vulnerability is the grid recovery mechanism. Gold frequently moves in long trends without short-term pullbacks. A heavy one-way move will force the robot to open large lots. This action exposes the account to sudden margin calls. The lack of standard protective stops heightens this systemic vulnerability.

Traders must implement severe risk management rules when using this software. Testing the settings on a demo account for 30 days is mandatory. Users must select top-tier ECN brokers with tight execution windows. Depositing only disposable capital and withdrawing profits weekly helps prevent total account blow-out scenarios.

Final Verdict

Scalping Robot Pro MT5 delivers high-speed automation with extensive market filters. Yet, the underlying grid system requires expert supervision. It is not suitable for beginners who do not understand martingale risks. Experienced traders must manage leverage carefully to survive volatile gold extensions.
