Overview

The Order Flow Full Suite is an institutional-grade volume tracking toolkit engineered to reveal aggressive buyer and seller market orders directly inside the candlesticks. Developed exclusively as a native MT5 indicator, it eliminates lagging technical metrics by providing an unfiltered, real-time footprint chart. Optimized by eafree.net for rapid intra-day execution on lower timeframes ranging from M1 to M15, this system delivers unmatched trade-entry timing. It acts as a powerful execution trigger across highly liquid assets, specifically XAUUSD (Gold), Crypto, and major Forex pairs.

How Does Order Flow Full Suite Work?

Retail indicators fail because they calculate past closing prices. Order Flow Full Suite operates on a completely different level, drawing raw, tick-by-tick transaction data directly via the native MetaTrader 5 CopyTicks function. It decodes real-time market mechanics by analyzing how orders cross the spread:

  • Aggressive Buyers: Market buy orders executing directly into the Ask price.
  • Aggressive Sellers: Market sell orders executing directly into the Bid price.

The data processing infrastructure automatically adapts based on your specific broker feed:

  • Real Volume: 100% accurate, centralized exchange data. This is activated automatically on Futures, Equities, and centralized Crypto feeds.
  • Tick Rule: Deployed on decentralized Forex CFD networks where global volume is unavailable. The software uses a highly optimized Lee-Ready proxy algorithm (Uptick = Buy, Downtick = Sell). The official eafree.net structural manual notes that tick rule volume is a relative proxy. Traders should scale down nominal position sizes by 30% to account for broker spread widening.

The tool structures this raw data into 5 core order flow components:

  • Footprint Chart: A real-time matrix mapping the exact numbers of "Sellers × Buyers" at every specific price increment inside a single candle.
  • Delta: The net difference between aggressive buying volume and aggressive selling volume within an individual candle.
  • Cumulative Delta (CVD): The accumulated summation of Delta across 50+ rolling bars, showing whether institutions are accumulating or distributing.
  • Volume Imbalance: Triggered when diagonal buying volume overpowers selling volume (or vice versa) by a strict customizable ratio (Default ≥ 3×).
  • POC (Point of Control): The single price level containing the highest volume density in a candle, marked by a bright yellow line that functions as a high-liquidity magnet.

Key Features of Order Flow Full Suite

  • Granular Input Controls: Dictate exactly how the market grid renders through highly responsive parameter inputs, including RowPoints (price step configuration), DeltaThr (delta threshold spikes), and ImbRatio (imbalance multiplier sensitivity).
  • 5 Automated Order Flow Signals (Non-Repainting):
    • Aggressive Buyers/Sellers: Flags momentum acceleration when Delta reaches ≥ ±25%, closing near price extremes (≥ 70% or ≤ 30%) with volume exceeding 1.2× the moving average.
    • Absorption: Identifies institutional limit orders swallowing up market execution. Marked by an asset diamond icon when a heavy Delta spike fails to push price further, forcing an opposite-direction close.
    • Exhaustion: Displays structural fatigue when footprint extremes show extremely low volume (≤ 15% of average), revealing that the drive has run out of participants.
    • Divergence: Exposes a weakening trend by flagging immediate visual divergences between raw price action and rolling Delta/CVD momentum.
    • Stacked Imbalance: Automatically color-codes zones where ≥ 3 consecutive layers of diagonal imbalances align, constructing a hard institutional defense line.
  • Real-Time HUD Dashboard: A highly interactive control panel pinned directly to the chart, displaying live volume feed status (Real Volume / Tick Rule), current CVD direction, and daily signal aggregations.
  • Instant Alert System: Instantly forwards critical trading triggers directly to your desktop terminal, mobile app push notifications, or custom Telegram bot channels.

Performance & Backtest Results

As an advanced execution suite, Order Flow Full Suite functions as a decision-support semi-automated indicator rather than a hands-free, automated black-box EA. Consequently, automated MT5 .htm strategy tester reports are not applicable. Instead, its performance is measured by how effectively it hardens macro setups like SMC (Smart Money Concepts), Wyckoff, or ICT.

When deployed as a mechanical filter at high-timeframe Point of Interests (POI), the suite delivers substantial statistical advantages:

  • Maximized Risk-to-Reward (R:R): Instead of using wide structural stop-losses, order flow allows you to enter with ultra-tight stops — measuring just 0.2 to 0.3 ATR right beneath the confirmation footprint. This routinely scales trade R:R ratios from a standard 1:1 up to 1:3 or 1:5.
  • Hardened Win Rates: By verifying institutional volume entry at key Order Blocks (OB) or Spring zones, you completely eliminate guessing games. Waiting for an aggressive candle confirmation before pulling the trigger reduces false breakouts and premature stop-outs by up to 35% to 40%.

Weaknesses and Risk Warnings

  • Forex Spread Noise: During high-impact news releases or weekly market openings, brokers aggressively widen spreads. This introduces statistical noise into Tick Rule calculations, causing minor mathematical variances in absolute Delta values.
  • Heavy Hardware Demands: Processing massive data streams via CopyTicks forces heavy RAM and CPU usage. Running this intensive suite across too many assets simultaneously on lower-end systems can cause chart lag.
  • No-Man's-Land Traps: Order flow signals printed in the middle of a trading range or without a higher-timeframe anchor are highly prone to failure. You must align these micro-triggers with macro structure.

Technical Setup & Installation Guide

Deploying the Order Flow Full Suite requires exact directory placement within MetaTrader 5 to handle the tick data stream efficiently.

➡️ Step 1: Deploy Files to MT5 Directory

  1. Open your MetaTrader 5 desktop platform.
  2. Click File in the top menu and select Open Data Folder.
  3. Navigate through the following path: MQL5 ➡️ Indicators.
  4. Paste your indicator file directly into this folder.

➡️ Step 2: Refresh and Launch the Suite

  1. Return to MT5 and open the Navigator panel (Ctrl + N).
  2. Right-click Indicators and click Refresh (or restart MT5 entirely).
  3. Launch a clean chart of your target asset (e.g., XAUUSD) on a low timeframe (M1, M5, or M15).
  4. Drag and drop the indicator from the Navigator window onto your active chart.

➡️ Step 3: Critical Platform Authorization

Before clicking OK, you must grant administrative permissions within the indicator settings window:

  1. Navigate to the Common tab.
  2. Check the box next to "Allow DLL imports".
  • Crucial Rule: This step is mandatory. Without DLL access, the software cannot process advanced calculation routines, update web data, or route trade alerts to your personal Telegram bot.
  1. Click OK to finish. The platform will automatically optimize your workspace into a sleek Dark Pro theme.

➡️ Step 4: Interface Customization

  • Dashboard HUD: The control hub locks onto the top-left corner by default. Click and hold the header title bar to drag and position the HUD anywhere on your monitor. The position is saved for that specific chart.
  • Language Toggle: Click the EN/VI button located on the dashboard footer to instantly toggle the interface language globally across all active charts.
  • Activating the Grid View: To see raw numbers inside individual candles, click the ShowFootprint toggle on the HUD and maximize your chart zoom level (+ key).
