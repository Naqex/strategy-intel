FAQ

High Frequency Trading Bot MT4 uses ultra-fast scalping logic with very tight order distance and rapid execution, targeting small price movements during short trading windows.

High Frequency Trading Bot MT4 applies a small fixed lot and tight stop loss (10 points), but lacks dynamic risk control, so proper lot sizing is essential for safety.

High Frequency Trading Bot MT4 performs best on low spread ECN brokers during high liquidity hours (16:00–22:00) with fast execution and minimal slippage.

High Frequency Trading Bot MT4 can be used by beginners, but due to its sensitivity to spread and latency, it requires VPS hosting and broker optimization.
